Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 September 2026

What this website collects

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. It sets no identifiers in your browser and does not build a profile of your visit.

The enquiry form

The enquiry form does not transmit anything on its own. When you submit it, your device opens your own email application with the details you entered already written into a message addressed to us. Nothing is sent until you send that email yourself.

What we do with your enquiry

If you write to us by email, WhatsApp or telephone, we keep your name and contact details so that we can answer you and follow up about the residences. We use them for that purpose only. We do not sell them and we do not pass them to anyone outside our team and the Homeland Park Collection partnership.

Hosting

Our hosting provider records standard server logs, including the internet address your request came from, for security and reliability. We do not use those logs to identify visitors.

Your choices

Ask us at any time to see the details we hold about you, correct them, or delete them. Write to enquiry@homelandglobalpark.com and we will act on your request.
